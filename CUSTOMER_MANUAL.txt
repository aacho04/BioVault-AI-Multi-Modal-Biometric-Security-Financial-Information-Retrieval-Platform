1. PLATFORM OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
BioVault-AI is an enterprise-grade financial intelligence and banking vault 
combining zero-trust passwordless authentication with real-time deep learning 
fraud screening.

Key Protection Pillars:
- Multi-Modal Zero-Trust Security: Email OTP + FIDO2 Passkeys + 128-D Face Cards.
- Deep Learning Autoencoder: Screens every transaction feature (amount, velocity, 
  hour, distance) to detect fraudulent anomalies.
- Strict Biometric Immutability: Enrolled facial templates are cryptographically 
  hashed with RS256 token verification.
- Instant Lockdown Shield: Prevents brute-force or unauthorized face scans by 
  blocking the website right away after multiple failed attempts.

2. GETTING STARTED: FIRST-TIME SETUP & LOGIN
================================================================================
Access the application by navigating to http://localhost:3000 in your browser.

2.1. Email One-Time Password (OTP):
     - Enter your registered email address (e.g., yourname@domain.com).
     - Click "Send Verification Code".
     - Check your inbox for the 6-digit numeric code.
     - Enter the 6 digits and click "Verify Email OTP".

2.2. FIDO2 / WebAuthn Hardware Passkey:
     - Click "Register FIDO2 Hardware Passkey".
     - Your operating system / browser will prompt for biometric confirmation 
       (Windows Hello, Touch ID, Fingerprint, or USB Security Key).
     - Once approved, hardware credentials are cryptographically bound.

2.3. Security Transaction PIN Setup:
     - Set a 4-to-6 digit private numeric PIN (default demo PIN: 1234).
     - Re-enter to confirm and save your PIN.
     - This PIN is required to authorize all outgoing financial transfers.

2.4. 128-D Biometric Facial Enrollment:
     - Allow browser webcam permissions when prompted.
     - Position your face directly in the center of the dashed oval HUD.
     - Ensure adequate frontal lighting and look directly into the camera lens.
     - Click "Enroll Face Template".
     - The engine extracts a normalized 128-dimensional embedding vector and locks 
       it under the 180-Day Biometric Immutability Policy.

3. DAILY OPERATIONS & CORE FEATURES
================================================================================

3.1. Account Balance & Liquidity Overview:
     - Your real-time available funds (in ₹ INR) are displayed on the Dashboard.
     - Recent transactions show real-time clearing statuses, merchants, and AI 
       reconstruction error logs.

3.2. Sending Wire Transfers (₹ INR):
     - Scroll to "Simulate a Secure Wire Transfer".
     - Choose a merchant or clearing institution.
     - Enter transfer amount (₹) and transaction velocity.
     - Click "Proceed to Transfer Authorization".
     - Enter your 4-digit Security PIN in the verification modal.

3.3. AI Autoencoder Fraud Detection & Anomaly Scoring:
     - Every transfer is evaluated by a Python Deep Autoencoder microservice.
     - Normal baseline transfers yield a low reconstruction error (L_rec < 0.45) 
       and clear immediately upon PIN entry.
     - High-risk transfers (unusual surge amounts, high velocity spikes, or 
       atypical hours) are flagged with high loss (L_rec >= 0.45).

3.4. High-Value Step-Up Biometric Face Challenge:
     - Whenever a transfer meets or exceeds your High-Value Trigger Threshold 
       (default ₹2,000.00), or triggers an AI anomaly:
     - The platform automatically enforces Stage 2: Biometric Face Scan.
     - The webcam will activate. Look directly into the cyan oval and click 
       "Scan Face & Settle Funds".
     - Funds are released ONLY when the face matches the registered account owner.

3.5. Customizing High-Value Biometric Trigger Limits:
     - In the Dashboard top banner, click "Set High-Value Limit".
     - Enter your preferred threshold in ₹ INR (e.g., ₹5,000 or ₹10,000).
     - Click "Scan Face to Confirm Limit Change".
     - Face verification is mandatory to prevent unauthorized limit tampering.

3.6. Updating Your Security PIN:
     - In the Dashboard top banner, click "Update Security PIN".
     - Complete the 3-step verification:
       1. Enter Email OTP sent to your registered inbox.
       2. Scan your face to verify account ownership.
       3. Enter and confirm your new 4-to-6 digit PIN.

4. FACIAL BIOMETRIC AUTHENTICATION GUIDELINES
================================================================================
To ensure seamless verification and prevent false mismatches, follow these 
recommended practices:

4.1. Camera Alignment & Cyan Guide Oval:
     - Sit centered in front of your camera.
     - Keep your entire face (forehead, eyes, nose, cheeks, mouth, jawline) 
       inside the cyan dashed oval displayed on the video feed.
     - Avoid sitting off to the side, as the scanner crops the center region.

4.2. Proper Lighting, Distance, and Gaze:
     - Ensure bright frontal lighting on your face.
     - Avoid strong overhead or behind-the-back light sources that cast dark shadows.
     - Look directly into the camera lens at eye level.
     - Keep an approximate distance of 1.5 to 2.5 feet (45–75 cm) from the camera.

4.3. Understanding the Euclidean Distance Metric:
     - The biometric engine calculates the distance D between your live face and 
       the enrolled template:
       D = sqrt( sum( (v_enrolled[i] - v_live[i])^2 ) )
     - A genuine matching face typically scores D between 0.10 and 0.35.
     - Security Match Threshold: D < 0.55.
     - If D >= 0.55, the attempt is rejected as an unauthorized mismatch.

5. SECURITY INCIDENT PROTOCOL & ACCOUNT LOCKDOWN
================================================================================

5.1. Failed Authentication Thresholds:
     - If face authentication or PIN entry fails, an attempt counter is tracked.
     - If biometric authentication fails 3 consecutive times, the system 
       immediately engages emergency lockdown.

5.2. Instant On-Screen Website Block:
     - The website is blocked right away on your active screen.
     - No external redirects or URL forwarding occur.
     - A full-screen security lockdown alert appears:
       "🛑 SECURITY LOCKDOWN: WEBSITE BLOCKED"
     - Outgoing transfers, logins, and settings changes are completely frozen.
     - New account registrations are strictly disabled.

5.3. Unblocking Your Website (Face Authentication ONLY):
     - There is NO bypass button or generic reset code.
     - To lift the lockdown, the legitimate account owner must verify via 
       Face Authentication only:
       1. Click "Start Camera" on the lockdown screen.
       2. Center your face in the cyan oval HUD.
       3. Click "Authenticate Face to Unblock".
       4. Once genuine identity is verified (D < 0.55), the lockdown is lifted 
          instantly right where you are, and normal operations resume.

5.4. Email Security Alert & 5-Hour Freeze:
     - Upon unauthorized face detection, an incident forensics email is sent to 
       your registered email address.
     - It details the timestamp, transaction action, and biometric mismatch score.
     - If you suspect an intruder, the email includes a 1-click 5-hour freeze 
       protection link to lock the account.
